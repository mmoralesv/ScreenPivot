## Product name

ScreenPivot

## Short description (max. 1,000 characters)

Working in a full-screen Cloud PC or remote desktop? ScreenPivot alerts you about what happens in your local apps (mail, chats, calls, builds) without leaving the session, and takes you to the app in one click.

## Description (max. 10,000 characters)

When you work in a full-screen Cloud PC or remote desktop, your own computer's desktop is hidden and Windows silences its notifications. A call, an urgent message or a finished build can go unnoticed for hours.

ScreenPivot watches the apps on your computer while you are in the remote session and alerts you with a discreet banner on top of it, without taking focus or the keyboard away.

ALERTS BY WHAT MATTERS
• Reads your apps' notifications, including the ones Windows silences in full screen.
• Ranks them Critical, High, Normal or Low by your rules: VIP senders, keywords, per-app rules and muted apps.
• Critical: banner stays until you close it. High: banner for a few seconds. Everything else adds to a counter.
• A different sound for each priority: Windows sounds, your own WAV file, or none.

APPS WITHOUT NOTIFICATIONS TOO
• Detects when an app flashes its taskbar button to ask for attention.
• Title rules: alert when text like "Build succeeded" appears or a counter like "(3)" goes up.

SWITCH SCREENS IN ONE CLICK
• "Open" takes you to the app (or the exact window) behind the alert.
• "Back to the Cloud PC" returns you to the session.
• Choose whether the app appears on top of the session or the remote desktop is minimized.

SUMMARY WHEN YOU RETURN
• When you leave the remote session, see what happened, grouped by app and sorted by importance.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything happens on your device: no account, no servers, no ads, no telemetry.
• The history is stored encrypted for your Windows user and deleted automatically (24 hours by default).

Works with Windows App (Windows 365 / Cloud PC) and other Windows Remote Desktop clients. ScreenPivot is an independent app and is not affiliated with Microsoft.

The app interface is currently in Spanish.
